Add a race

Paste the results link from the race's own website. A bare number is treated as an Athlinks event id; use series:12345 for a recurring race. Adding a race switches to it and pulls its roster straight away.

No internet? Bring your own roster

A CSV needs a bib column and a name column (or first and last name). Also understood: age, gender, city, state, country, team, race/course, time, place. The demo race has 420 made-up runners and works with the demo camera.

Accuracy self-test

Renders synthetic bibs from the active race's numbers, abuses them the way a finish line does (angle, blur, noise, JPEG) and measures what the scanner makes of them. Scanning stops while it runs.
